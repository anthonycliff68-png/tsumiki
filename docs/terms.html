Terms and Community Rules

Tsumiki Last updated: 30 September 2026

Tsumiki is a habit app you use with a small group of friends. These terms say what you can expect from it, what it expects from you, and what happens when someone behaves badly. Like the privacy policy, it is written plainly on purpose.

Who provides it

Tsumiki is made by Anthony Clifford, an individual developer. For anything on this page, write to anthonycliff68@gmail.com.

Using Tsumiki

You need an account, and you must be old enough to have one where you live. One account is yours alone — do not share it, and do not sign in as someone else.

You keep everything you put into Tsumiki. Your habits, your routine and your check-ins are yours. We store them so the app works and so they follow you to a new phone; we do not sell them and we do not use them to advertise to you.

You can delete your account from inside the app, under You. It removes your profile, habits, routine, check-ins, nudges and crew memberships. It cannot be undone.

Community rules

Crews are small and invite-only, but anyone you invite can see your display name, your habit and whether you have checked in — and can send you a nudge. So there are rules.

Nudges are for encouragement. A nudge is a short message asking someone to do the thing they already said they would do. It is not for insults, pressure about anything else, or contacting somebody who has asked you to stop.

Do not use Tsumiki to harass anyone. That includes threats, abuse, hate, sexual content, or targeting a person over who they are. It also includes using a display name or habit name to say something you could not say in a nudge.

Do not impersonate people. Use a name your crew would recognise as you.

Do not try to break it. No scraping, no automated accounts, no attempting to reach other people's data.

Blocking and reporting

You can block anyone you share a crew with, from their tile on the crew screen. Blocking cuts both directions: they cannot nudge you, you stop receiving anything they have already sent, and neither of you can reach the other. It does not tell them.

You can report a nudge, a crew or a profile from the same place. Reports are read and acted on within 24 hours, and the person reported is not told who reported them.

Acting on a report can mean removing content, removing someone from a crew, or removing their account. Accounts removed for harassing people do not come back.

Subscriptions

Tsumiki is free to start. If a paid subscription is offered, it is an auto-renewing subscription bought through your Apple account.

Payment is taken at confirmation of purchase. It renews automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your Apple account is charged for renewal within 24 hours of the end of that period. You can manage or cancel it in your Apple account settings; deleting the app does not cancel it.

Any unused part of a free trial is forfeited when you buy a subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us.

What Tsumiki does not promise

It is a habit app, not a medical or mental health service. It does not give health advice, and nothing in it should be used in place of talking to a professional.

We try hard to keep your data safe and available, and it is backed up — but the app is provided as it is, and we cannot guarantee it will never be unavailable or never lose anything. Nothing here takes away rights you have by law where you live.

Ending it

You can stop using Tsumiki at any time and delete your account from inside the app. We can suspend or remove an account that breaks the community rules above, and we will say why unless doing so would put someone at risk.

Changes

If these terms change in a way that matters, the app will say so before the change takes effect. The date at the top is always the date of the version you are reading.
