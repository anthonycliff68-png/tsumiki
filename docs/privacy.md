# Privacy Policy

**Tsumiki**
Last updated: 25 September 2026

Tsumiki is a habit app you use with a small group of friends. This policy
says what it stores, why, who can see it, and how to get rid of it. It is
written plainly on purpose.

## Who is responsible

Tsumiki is made by Anthony Clifford, an individual developer. For anything
in this policy, including a request to delete your data, write to
**anthonycliff68@gmail.com**.

## What Tsumiki stores

**Your account.** Your email address, so you can sign in. If you use Sign in
with Apple and choose to hide your email, Apple gives us a relay address
instead and we never see your real one.

**Your profile.** A display name, a colour, your time zone, and the hours you
have asked not to be disturbed. Your time zone is needed because a habit is
due on *your* local date, not ours.

**Your routine and habits.** The moments of your day (wake up, lunch, and so
on), the habits you stack onto them, the days of the week each one runs, and
the times you expect to do them.

**Your check-ins.** Which habit you completed and on which of your local
dates. This is what streaks are counted from.

**Your crews.** Which crews you belong to, who else is in them, and invites
you have created or accepted.

**Nudges and reactions.** The nudges you send and receive, including the
short message attached, and any thank-you reaction.

**Reports and blocks.** If you block someone or report them, we store that,
because it would not work otherwise.

**A push token**, if you allow notifications, so reminders and nudges can
reach your device. Delete the app or turn notifications off and it stops
being used.

Tsumiki has no advertising, no analytics SDK, no third-party trackers, and
no profiling. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone for
marketing.

## Who can see your information

**Your crew can see** your display name, your colour, whether you have
checked in today, your current streak state, and nudges between you. They
**cannot** see your email address, your other habits, your routine, or any
crew you are in that they are not.

**Nobody else can see anything.** There are no public profiles and no way to
search for a person. Access is enforced in the database itself, per row, not
only in the app.

## Who processes it for us

**Supabase** hosts the database, authentication and server functions.
**Expo** operates the push notification service that delivers reminders and
nudges. **Apple** provides Sign in with Apple and app distribution. Each one
only handles what it needs to do that job.

## How long it is kept

Until you delete it. Delete your account in the app (You → Delete my
account) and your profile, habits, check-ins, nudges and crew memberships
are removed. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Crew streaks that
other people share are not reset by your leaving.

## Your rights

You can see everything Tsumiki holds about you inside the app, correct it
there, and delete all of it there. If you would rather have a copy sent to
you, or want anything removed by hand, email the address above and it will
be dealt with within 30 days. Depending on where you live you may also have
the right to object to processing or to complain to a data protection
regulator.

## Children

Tsumiki is not intended for children under 13 and is not directed at them.

## Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Row-level security rules mean one account
cannot read another's rows even if the app asked it to. No system is
perfect, and this policy does not promise otherwise.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes
and the app will say so before the change takes effect.
