# Tsumiki — Support

Tsumiki builds habits with your people, one stack at a time.

## Get help

Email **anthonycliff68@gmail.com**. Include your device, your iOS version,
and what you expected to happen. Most replies come within a couple of days.

## Common questions

**I never got my sign-in code.** Check the spam folder first. Codes last one
hour and each new one replaces the last, so use the most recent email. If
several requests come back refused, wait a few minutes — there is a limit on
how often a code can be sent to the same address.

**How does the streak work?** A crew shares one streak, and the rule is
never miss twice. Missing a scheduled day once is forgiven and the streak
carries on. Missing again before you check in resets it to zero. Checking in
clears the forgiveness for next time.

**Why is my habit due on a different day to my friend's?** Everyone in a
crew does the same habit, but each person picks their own moment and their
own days. Dates are always counted in your own time zone.

**Can I stop the notifications?** Yes — in iOS Settings under Tsumiki, or
set quiet hours in the app so nothing arrives while you are asleep.

**How do I delete my account?** In the app: You → Delete my account. It
removes your profile, habits, check-ins and crew memberships straight away
and cannot be undone.
