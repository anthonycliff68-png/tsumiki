## Description (4000 max)
Tsumiki is a habit app built on two ideas that actually work: attach a new
habit to something you already do, and do it with people who notice when you
don't.

STACK IT ONTO YOUR DAY
You set up your day once — wake up, coffee, lunch, home, bed. Those are your
anchors. Every habit hangs off one of them. "After lunch, a fifteen minute
walk" is a plan. "Walk more" is a wish. You can also set a habit to a time,
or leave it as anytime today.

DO IT WITH A CREW
Add two to five friends to a habit and you share one streak. Everyone does
the same habit, but each person picks their own moment — your walk can be
after lunch and theirs after work. The streak belongs to all of you.

NEVER MISS TWICE
Missing one day is forgiven. The streak survives. Miss again before you check
in and it resets. It's the rule that keeps a bad week from undoing a good
month, and it's why the streak still means something.

NUDGE, DON'T NAG
If someone hasn't checked in, send a nudge — once a day, and only if they
actually still owe the day. It arrives at the moment they meant to do the
thing, never in the middle of the night, and never after they've already
done it. They can check in straight from the notification, or say they're
heading out now, which quiets everything for half an hour.

SEE WHERE IT'S GOING
A day is a set of rings. A week is a wall you can read at a glance. A month
is a line. "Needs work" and "Going well" are separate, so the habit that's
slipping is the first thing you see — with one suggestion for what to do
about it, like dropping the weekday that keeps breaking it, or stacking a
habit that's been floating loose.

Tsumiki has no feed, no followers, no likes, and no way for a stranger to
find you. It's built for a group small enough to care.

## Notes to reviewer
Tsumiki does not use passwords. To sign in, tap "Sign in with Apple" on the
first screen and use any Apple ID, including a test account. This creates a
fresh account and takes you straight into onboarding.

The email option sends a six-digit code to the address you enter, so it needs
an inbox you control; Sign in with Apple avoids that entirely and is the
quickest way in.

WHAT TO TRY
1. Sign in with Apple.
2. Onboarding sets up the moments of your day, then you pick a first habit.
   Choose "Start solo" at the crew step.
3. On Today, the habits left are held as a fan of cards. Swipe sideways
   through them. Tap a card to check it in; tap again to undo.
4. My Day shows the same habits on a timeline against your routine.
5. Progress shows rings for a day, a heat wall for a week, a trend for a
   month. A new account has no history yet, so these start empty and fill in
   as you check things off.

CREWS AND NUDGES
A crew needs a second real person, so it cannot be fully exercised from one
device. Create a crew from Crews > New, and the invite screen shows the link
you would send. Nudges only appear once a crew has another member who has
not yet checked in.

NOTIFICATIONS
The app asks for notification permission during onboarding. Reminders fire at
each habit's expected time, so nothing will arrive during a short review
session. Declining permission does not block any other feature.

ACCOUNT DELETION
You > Delete my account removes everything immediately.
